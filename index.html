About our restaurant

At Vaishno Bhojnalaya, culinary tradition meets wholesome hospitality. Rooted in the rich heritage of North Indian vegetarian gastronomy, our chefs craft authentic homestyle dishes, slow-simmered gravies, and fragrant tandoori flatbreads prepared fresh daily with uncompromising cleanliness.

Purity is our sacred standard. We use only farm-fresh seasonal produce, slow-roasted spices, and authentic desi ghee. Whether you are craving our signature Deluxe Thali, creamy Shahi Paneer, or slow-cooked Dal Makhani, every meal brings the soothing comfort of home.
